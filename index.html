About

Four decades of building things that ship.

John Hasson is an entrepreneur and software engineer based in Folsom, California. He started programming in 1984 and never stopped — a career arc that runs from early personal computing, through the Y2K remediation era, to today's generative-AI boom.

He works across an unusually wide range: designing autonomous AI agents and automation systems, building and shipping production software, researching the quantitative side of the markets (equities and options), and digging into application security. The common thread is a builder's bias — take something complicated and turn it into a tool people can actually use.

Through Hawthorne LLC, he builds CoCallers, an AI voice receptionist platform for property management and contractors. He also ships independent tools like Klunk (klunk.dev), which turns screen recordings into ready-to-use dev tickets, has spoken at industry conferences, and continues to invest in and tinker with emerging technology.
